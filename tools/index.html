The Method

The method, in order.

Seven steps, and everything on this site sits at exactly one of them. Name the problem, find out where you stand, check it against evidence, decide the forks, design and sequence the build, then prove it worked. Each step says what it hands to the next — nothing here is meant to be a destination.
