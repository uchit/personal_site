The problem

The bank had no shared way to archive data. Each application team had built its own, with its own tools, its own process and its own bill.

Regulation says some records must be kept for years. Because there was nowhere central to keep them, old systems stayed switched on long after anyone used them, just to hold their data.

What made it hard

  • Scale. More than 1,000 applications and several petabytes, spread across on-premises systems and several cloud regions.
  • Mixed data. Database tables, files, documents and logs all needed the same retention, privacy and audit controls.
  • Adoption. Application teams had to choose to move. If getting data back out was slow or awkward, they wouldn’t.

What we built

We spent three months assessing all 1,000+ applications before designing anything. We compared off-the-shelf products against a custom build on data patterns, compliance needs and cost. A custom AWS platform won.

We rolled it out by type of data rather than by application: structured data first, then documents and files. Retention, expiry and deletion run automatically. Teams get their data back through self-service, with access controlled by their existing Active Directory groups.

The bank’s own team runs it. We handed over full design documents, runbooks and review processes.
