About.

I’m a lead forward deployed engineer in Melbourne, focused on AI-led enterprise transformation. I work inside the delivery team and build the thing myself, usually against a date that can’t move.

I started in 2011 writing Java and running Linux servers in Ahmedabad. Cloud was new, so I wrote about it: five books for Apress and Packt between 2013 and 2016, mostly about AWS. Then came DevOps and SRE work for banks in Singapore and Thailand, enterprise architecture at KPMG Singapore, and platform and modernisation work across Australia and New Zealand.

Now I spend most of my time on AI in large enterprises: getting agents and AI services into production, and fixing the data and core platforms they depend on. That means giving an agent its own identity, limiting what it can do, keeping a record a regulator will accept, and still shipping on time.

I still build things myself, from prototypes with new AI tools to a product I run on my own. It keeps my advice honest. Outside work I lift weights and write about training after 35. I speak Gujarati, Hindi and English, and a little Spanish.
