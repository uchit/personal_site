Start from the problem

Named for what’s actually going wrong.

The sector views are for readers who already know they are a bank. These are for the ones who arrive with a problem instead — the four that recur most across engagements. Each names the symptoms, says what is usually actually wrong, and routes to the diagnostic, playbook or paved path that moves it.

Regulatory date · fixed scope · no target state

There’s a hard deadline and no architecture.

A date committed externally, and the design underneath it does not exist yet. What to decide up front, and what to defer on purpose.

Start here → Demo works · legal blocked · no path to production

The AI pilot works. Risk won’t sign it off.

The model works and the demo lands. It has been in front of risk for a quarter because nobody translated the obligation into engineering work.

Start here → Golden cage · low adoption · shadow tooling

You built a platform. Teams route around it.

Funded, built, and quietly bypassed. Adoption gets reported as a communications problem. It is not a communications problem.

Start here → No owner · no unit economics · finance asking

The cloud bill is growing faster than the business.

Finance is asking questions engineering cannot answer, and the last cost-cutting sprint bought three months before spend regrew.
